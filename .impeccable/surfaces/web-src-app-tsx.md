# Surface brief: stash web app (Files, Transfers, Watch)

Mode: operate. Audience: the owner on a Mac, phones over Tailscale/LAN, family on a TV in the living room in the evening with the lights low. Job: add a magnet, find a film, start it fast, manage transfers. Constraints: only real backend data (no invented dates, art, media info); MKV/HEVC playback and subtitles arrive in later items.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A projection booth at night: the interface is the dark warm room and the film is the light. Refuses the category default of a flat grey file manager with a blue accent, and refuses the poster-wall streaming clone.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black booth walls (#14110f ground, #1f1a17 panels, hairline #3a312a), off-white print text (#f3ece0), tungsten amber (#f0a33a) for the primary action, focus and progress only. Light comes from the screen: the playing film's sampled colour washes the player surround, header and rail edge. Reel rings for transfer progress. One workhorse sans with tabular figures; no display faces.

STORY: You see what is ready, what is streaming and what is queued at a glance, pick a film, and it plays at once while the room takes its colour.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rail left (Files, Transfers with active count). Top bar: search with / shortcut, Add magnet as the amber primary. Files: dense list with swatch, name, size, format, status; reel ring on streaming rows. Watch: player dominant at 16:9 with its light spilling into the room, actions rail right (Play, Download, Copy stream link, VLC playlist, Delete isolated).

FORM: Projection Booth, candidate 1 of 7 on the ordered list (the model pick, chosen by the owner over the rolled Reservation Chart). seed:b2cfe65d707738a7
